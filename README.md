# The Shippers Group

The Shippers Group is a leading national third-party logistics (3PL) company headquartered in Dallas, Texas. Founded in 1901, the company provides warehousing, co-packaging, fulfillment, and transportation management services across 12 locations totaling over 5 million square feet of space. The Shippers Group serves brand leaders and market innovators in industries including food, consumer packaged goods, and retail. The company was acquired by Kenco Group in December 2023.
